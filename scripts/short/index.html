Narcissus In The Mirror

This is a 30 pages short-film script telling the story of ...

Do you remember the feeling of the last several semi-conscious minutes before fully waking up, when the dream and reality are mixed together? NARCIS is a superstar. D-Day of his most important tour, his world starts to disappear; everything and every relationship of his plunges into chaos - his autographing session, his manager, his band, his girlfriend… Narcis believes it’s only a terrible dream, so he tries to wake himself up; but the more he struggles, the worse the situtation will become.
