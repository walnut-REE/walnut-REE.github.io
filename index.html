Hi, I’m Ree, a Ph.D. student at ShanghaiTech University Visual Intelligent Center (VIC), supervised by Prof. Jingyi Yu. Before that, I received my B.S degree from Dalian University of Technology, under the supervision of Prof. Xin Fan. My primary research interest is AI for Creativity, especially in 3D+ domain, exploring to combining the most advanced AI techs with artistic creativity.

I write movie scripts and research on street photography during spare time.
